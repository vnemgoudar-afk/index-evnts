A feast for every sense

More than a food festival. It's a flavour adventure.

Welcome to FOODIE FEST 2026, where food lovers come together to celebrate tastes, traditions and creativity from around the world. Discover vibrant food stalls, meet passionate chefs, enjoy live entertainment and make delicious memories with friends and family.

From sizzling street-food favourites to sweet treats and refreshing drinks, there's something exciting around every corner. The festival is designed for guests aged 12 and above.
